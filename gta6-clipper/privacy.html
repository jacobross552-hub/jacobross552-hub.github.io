GTA6 Clipper Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

GTA6 Clipper only uploads videos to YouTube channels its owner controls, using the YouTube Data API.

It uses the Google OAuth scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly only to upload videos and to confirm which channel it is posting to.

It does not collect, sell or share any user or viewer data, and it has no users besides its owner.

OAuth tokens are stored privately on the owner's own server and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

GTA6 Clipper's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy and the YouTube API Services Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy also applies.
